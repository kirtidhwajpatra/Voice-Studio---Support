Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026.

Voice Studio does not collect, sell, share, or transmit your personal information, recordings, or voice data to our servers or to third parties.

Recording, voice enhancement, and export processing happen locally on your device. Your recordings remain under your control in the app and in locations you choose when exporting or sharing them.

Voice Studio uses Apple’s in-app purchase system for optional Pro access. Purchase transactions are handled by Apple; Voice Studio does not receive or store your payment-card details.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.
